Frames are drawn looking right. Mirror pairs cover every delivered occupied frame; reflected right-side NPCs face inward in the camp compositions. Blank notices, ledger, measuring tape and pictorial map avoid text that would reverse. Only the requested six staff and the optional owl are new characters.

The runtime crop uses the same 512 px cell and y=496 sole convention as the accepted unit art. The lower planted area is held constant across a character's eleven frames. Four-slot idle/work loops use a return intermediate for the final slot and run at 8 fps. Changes are concentrated in the head, arms and small held props. Workstation paintings are separate from character atlases. The central firepit and hero spots remain clear in the proposed placement mocks.

Review images sample decoded runtime WebPs. The lineup places NPCs, Aegis and Lio on the same 256 px frame grid; the silhouette comparison uses exactly 90 px visible figure height for all sixteen figures. Portraits have warm painted backgrounds; all other runtime files have alpha, no painted ground or cast shadow. Glow is confined to coals and potion glass.

These are asset review compositions, not integrated application screenshots. Raster-generated costume microdetails and final subjective style/silhouette acceptance remain for Claude Asset Intake Review. No source code, public assets, gameplay, HUD layout or palette tokens are changed.
