# Built-in image-generation prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen`, two separate calls. Reference inputs are repository-local Fateweave artwork only. Requested canvas dimensions are guidance; actual source dimensions are recorded in NOTES.md. Packing and edge normalization happen after generation.

## Tile sheet — opaque background

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an ORIGINAL production game TILE ATLAS for Asterveil Fateweave dense forest. Reference images are style and palette references ONLY: existing tileset for camera and warm upper-left light; approved forest keyart for pale olive soil, deep teal vegetation, mossy limestone. Output exactly 1536x1152 if possible, a perfectly aligned 4 columns by 3 rows grid of square cells, NO gutters, borders, labels or text. Each cell is a flat square texture filling its entire cell, NOT isometric diamonds. Row 1: four subtly different walkable trail floor textures: pale sandy olive earth, tiny scattered weathered limestone chips and sparse olive moss, quiet open centers. Row 2: four subtly different deeper grove floor textures: muted olive-green soil, fine leaf litter, low moss, slightly cooler/darker than trail but CLEARLY lighter than walls. NO big roots or plants blocking floors. Row 3 column1 forest wall TOP: dense dark teal leafy canopy interwoven brown roots over mossy stone; column2 forest wall FACE: upper mossy leafy ledge and dark vertical craggy front-facing cliff in gentle 3/4 top-down camera; column3 forest wall INNER: dark teal shaded mossy rocks and tightly packed roots, impassable; column4 repeat wall top. Painterly clean heroic chibi JRPG asset polish, same camera and top-left light as existing atlas. Tile edges must be seamless and cross-compatible within each floor family, no square outlines or vignette per cell, no large objects touching floor edges. No scenery, characters, UI, logos, watermarks, lettering. This is an atlas of textures, not a map illustration.

References: `public/assets/world/tileset.webp`; approved `incoming/ART-WORLD-KEYART-001/keyart_2.png` from the keyart branch.

## Prop sheet — transparent background

Use case stylized-concept. ORIGINAL production transparent game prop atlas, 4 columns by 2 rows equally spaced square cells. Eight individual isolated forest props, one per cell, NO text, NO grid lines. Each prop fully inside its cell with generous 10% empty margins and bottom-center ground contact at 90% cell height. Genuine transparent background, including arch opening. Top-down gentle 3/4 camera, warm upper-left illumination, clean hand-painted chibi JRPG polish matching reference decor; muted forest palette from approved keyart: dark teal leaves #24515A, olive moss #64724A, pale limestone #D2C79D, brown bark, cyan #74D4CF and tiny violet corruption. Row1 left-to-right: twisted spreading tree roots; twisted THORN ARCH with clearly empty open middle and two grounded feet; cluster of luminous TEAL shelf fungus; mossy FALLEN LOG lying diagonally with visible cut end. Row2: LANTERN MOSS clump with dangling softly glowing cyan seed pods (plant not manufactured lantern); RIFT-TOUCHED STUMP with small violet fissure in wood; graceful FERN CLUMP; upright weathered pale STONE MARKER with carved abstract diamond indentation and olive moss. No ground square or scenery backdrops, no characters, UI, logos or watermark. Props must remain identifiable at 36-48px. All art newly created.

References: `public/assets/world/decor.webp`; approved `incoming/ART-WORLD-KEYART-001/landmarks_2.png` from the keyart branch.
