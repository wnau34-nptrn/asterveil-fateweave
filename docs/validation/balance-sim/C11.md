## Decisions relative to Claude's WIP

- Follow `sigilward` (second proposal) and conditional `songnote` damage from the continuation handoff.
- Shadow cover lasts until the giver's next turn: expiring at the receiver's turn would erase the protection before many attacks. Own Shadow Step still expires at its own next turn. No dodge stacking is granted.
- Knight's now-unused Magic Power stat node becomes HP, preserving its +5% magnitude.
- Character gate uses actual map traversal at ★1, boss level (3/6/10), common epic map-level main-stat gear without sets or tomes. Best tested legal tree/loadout is picked from 6 branch-priority/choice variants and legal three-skill combinations using a separate boss probe. This is a reproducible raised-character scenario, not a claim of exhaustive global optimization or an ungeared first-run target. The first-run campaign remains a separate check.
- New gear preference data lives in src/content/heroes/balance.ts; simulations enumerate the hero catalog, with a data-based fallback for new entries. Character report groups by role (class) and stable character ID.
